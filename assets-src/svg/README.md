Everything here and every file it describes is regenerated by `npm run images` (`scripts/images/build-images.mjs`,
task `svg`); nothing is drawn by hand in an editor, so every shape is reproducible from the numbers below.

## Crest 丸に三つ組子

Ring between R and 0.84 R; three kumiko strips (width 0.11 R) on the equilateral triangle inscribed in 0.52 R, each
running 0.18 R past both vertices; half-laps alternate (pinwheel): strip *i* lies over at its first vertex and under at
its second, the under strip cut with a 0.035 R hairline of ground on both sides (the 割り crest painters use to show one
lath on another). The triangle stands on a vertex (▽) so the mark reads as a lattice joint, never as a letter A.
Construction order for the cover animation (`order`): circles → radii → intersections (magenta "intersect") → anchors →
fill. The favicon PNGs use an optically corrected variant (ring 0.8 R, strips 0.15 R, gap 0.045 R) for 16–32 px.

## Seal 「湊」 (白文, 小篆)

Reference: Wikimedia Commons, [File:湊-seal.svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:%E6%B9%8A-seal.svg)
(Shuowen seal script series; category "PD ancient script"), which cites the 說文解字 small-seal sources
[Sinica 小學堂 字形演變](http://xiaoxue.iis.sinica.edu.tw/yanbian?char=%E6%B9%8A), [hanziyuan.net](https://hanziyuan.net/#%E6%B9%8A),
[CUHK Multi-function Chinese Character Database](https://humanum.arts.cuhk.edu.hk/Lexis/lexi-mf/search.php?word=%E6%B9%8A) and
[ctext.org](https://ctext.org/dictionary.pl?if=en&char=%E6%B9%8A). The 22 strokes were traced as centre lines on that
drawing's 300-unit grid — 氵: the long S-shaped stream and four side strokes; 奏: 屮 sprout, the two hands of 廾 as claws,
bars and long arms, 夲 = 大 over 十 — then widened 1.32× to fill a single-character rectangular face (82 × 100),
the 水 strokes, long arms and 十 drawn down to the lower border (印化), and re-stroked at one even width (4.7 of 82 units, so the cut strokes hold their own against the red field down to 56 px) with round
ends (玉箸篆). 白文: the field prints, the strokes are cut away (left as paper). The face edge is a deterministic
hand-cut wobble with eased corners. `seal-paths.json` gives the carve module filled paths (`face` minus `glyph`).
The 肖形印 crest seal (`seal-crest.svg`) is a 朱文 square: frame and crest raised.

## Byōbu atlas

Atlas column *i* (left → right) = 第(*i*+1)扇 = case panel *i*+1: ledger, tracing, mapgl, strangler, platform,
resilience. Each panel is a 1 : 2.75 crop (512 × 1408; low tier 256 × 704) from inside one painted panel of the
photographed screen, at least 3 px clear of the paper-hinge seams and the brocade border (checked with edge
luminance profiles of the masters). Alpha is a pigment mask: Lab distance (L weighted ½) to the median gold ground of
that crop below `groundThreshold` → 0, ramping to 1 over `ramp` units; despeckled with a 1 px opening and softened to a
2 px edge, so the shader's gold leaf shows where the screen is bare and the paint stays matte. `erase` rects clear
writing on the gold (Mitsumochi's signature and seal). Encoded WebP q 72 / alpha 70 (visually identical to q 84 at half
the bytes: 0.5 MB / 0.3 MB). Mobile panels are opaque 1 : 1.6 crops at full width of the same single panel (no
seams), placed vertically by `mobileY`, always 560 × 896.

## Textures

- `gold-leaf-1280.avif`: 10 × 10 leaves of 128 px in staggered rows; per-leaf tone ±3 % and a sheen from its own
  angle; 箔足 overlaps (3 px, slightly darker, bright cut edge); fine laying creases and a soft mottle. Tileable.
- `washi-512.avif`: pale kōzo fibres (wavy, tapering, varying width, splatted with a soft footprint) in a cloudy
  formation (地合い) over a faint cool tooth; transparent, for the film overlay (normal blend).
- `grain-{day,night}-256.webp`: fine paper tooth pre-tinted 紺 / 月白; the night tile adds a few pale fibres.

## Today's print

`public/print/plate-{1..8}.svg` (viewBox 0 0 1240 1824). Plates 2–7 multiply in order, plate 8 (gold) is drawn
normally on top (opaque metallic ink, printed last), then the marbling clipped to the cartouche, then plate 1. The
cartouche is reserved on every block (clip = sheet minus cartouche): only its plate-6 ground and the name print there.
すやり霞 bands thin out at both ends like a drawn-out stroke; the gold pairs sit in the dawn sky and on the bare paper
beside the river, never over the green. Geometry for the compositor: `print-layout.json`.

## Favicons

`favicon.svg` switches ink for dark schemes (`prefers-color-scheme`). The raster tab icon (`favicon-32.png`) cannot,
so it carries a 胡粉 paper disc behind the optically corrected mark and stays legible on dark browser chrome.
