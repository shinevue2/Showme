## Deviations
- **Print paper:** today's print always uses the day 胡粉 paper, because artworks are never inverted at night (§3.6).
- **Notice:** the root-page notice sits as a small tag under the bar, and dismissing it stores `hanmoto.lang = 'ja'`.
- **Status bar:** the line is plain readouts; a separate "情報パネル ▴" button opens the sheet (needed for screen readers).
- **Chrome strings:** each page carries only its own language's strings, as inline JSON.
- **Focus order:** the Layers panel and status bar are moved after `<main>` at mount so focus goes doc bar → main → panels.
- **New page markers:** `html[data-chrome="ready"]` (for test scripts) and `data-unit` on the rulers.
- **Phone label:** the Separations toolbar button reads "Plates" in en, because "Separations" doesn't fit.

## Fixes made in `src/chrome/**`
- **CV menu and tooltips:** both drew under the top ruler. The doc bar now sits one layer above the rulers.
- **Info film readout:** it now reads WP-06's `debugFilm.plan` (variant and tier) instead of the video's src, which is a blob URL.
- **Settings → Quality:** choosing a quality without `?tier=` in the URL stored it but never reloaded. It now reloads, and the note says so.
- **Info frame-time graph:** the first frame after a rest was drawn as a huge slow frame. Those idle gaps are now dropped, an empty graph says "not drawing", and the canvas text has a CJK font.
- **Peek dialog:** it overflowed the status bar (the browser's default dialog height); it is now bounded. The footer was hard-coded English and is now translated; leading blank shader lines are trimmed.
- **Outline view on the road:** the `keyframe ch-N` label was hidden under the doc bar. The crossed box is now clipped to the reading area.
- **Year ruler:** the unit label `年` collided with the 2026 label. It moved to the 2018 end.
- **Notice:** "Also in English →" sat over the open Layers panel; it now hides while the panel is open.
- **Proof doc bar:** on en tablets (768–1000 px) the proof note ran off the right edge; it now hangs under the bar as a red tag. On phones the menu button stays at the edge.
- **Eye toggles:** their label changed between "Show" and "Hide" while `aria-pressed` also flipped. They now keep one name, "Show X", and only `aria-pressed` changes.
- **Plate rows:** plates not yet printed show an empty eye slot. In en, the plate name and the year/station now sit on two left-aligned lines.
- **Phone Layers sheet:** the express row repeated 履歴書 three times; it is now one CV line with three PDF links. The road row's expand arrow (twisty) was 14 px wide and is now 24 px.
- **Status bar:** it showed the English word "static" in ja/zh and "0 fps" before fps was measured. Both are fixed.
- **Glossary:** the reading (ruby) now shows in all three languages, not just ja.
- **Fonts:** the Layers panel footer (`.panel__foot`) used a mono font stack with no CJK face; it now uses the chrome stack.
- **Wording:** ja Info labels now read バリアント and コピー. zh now writes the Tōkaidō length as 一百二十六日里, because a Chinese 里 is 500 m and 一百二十六里 would read as 63 km. The 裏目 ruler label now reads 裏目 √2 (urame √2 in en).
- **Theme wipe:** the 本摺 ⇄ 石摺 wipe now passes through the bar and rulers instead of their snapping separately; I confirmed this on a mid-transition frame.
- **Smaller fixes:** instruments switch instantly when motion is off, the seal rotation is guarded against degree values, and the CV talk lines now have a separator before the venue.
