**What drives the scrub cost (R7, R8)**
- **A bug in `src/core/frame.ts` (WP-00).** Asking for a frame from inside a running frame starts a second, parallel animation-frame loop. Before my workaround, one 22 s scrub ran 7,752 player updates; after it, 196. I now defer my scroll callbacks and the player's frame requests until just after each frame. The chrome and other sections are still affected until WP-00 fixes it; the suggested one-line fix is in R7.
- **Page-wide work.** ScrollTrigger reads the scroll position right after Lenis scrolls, which forces style and layout (about 1 s per 12 s at 4×). The chrome rulers read `getBoundingClientRect` twice per frame after DOM writes and update `--mono-wdth` at 10 Hz: one scrub produced 424 layouts. Nothing in the road section forces layout per frame (checked with a hook).

**Deviations from the design**
- The section header is a framed title slip overlaid on the first film screen on landscape. That keeps the chapter-1 poster directly under the summary-road band for WP-05's match cut. On portrait and short landscape screens the header comes before the film instead.
- In film mode the `#road-N` ids move from the captions to markers at each chapter's rest. Deep links and the Layers links then land on the right frame; the static tier keeps the ids on the captions.
- Hidden captions are transparent (opacity 0) rather than `visibility: hidden`, so screen readers can read every chapter. Caption animations use only opacity and translate, so they run on the compositor.
- The print number moved into the reference print's credit line.
- On portrait, the scope and plate lines are visually hidden but stay readable to screen readers; the reference print is hidden and its credit kept. On ja laptop screens 820 px tall or less, the print image is hidden so the vertical column fits.
- At night the band behind the clouds continues the film's veiled paper, so the film ends at the artboard's edge rather than at the stage's bottom edge.
- The optional live-render toggle (生/版) is not built.

## Fixes (WP-06 files only)
1. **`scroll-film.ts`**: a seek now counts as in flight until its `seeked` event has been handled. Before, the next seek could start in the gap after `seeking` turned false, so only 13 of 50 seek completions were counted under load and the downgrade never fired. It now fires.
2. **`mount.ts`**:
   - The loader's promise kept the old file in memory after a variant swap; it is now released.
   - A failed swap used to leave a stray `<video>`, lose the object URL and could freeze the film. It now restores the previous state, with an 8 s timeout.
   - A stills fallback now disconnects the observer and clears its timers, and stops any load still in flight.
   - `debugFilm.plan` now shows `pendingVariant` and `swapping`.
3. **Rain**: the loop now runs only while the visitor scrolls through Shōno and stops 2.4 s after. The strokes keep their position in whole pixels. A running or even paused CSS loop, or a fractional offset, kept the GPU busy. The rain layers are now drawn smaller (about 45 % less area) and exist only inside the rain's range.
4. The night veil layer now exists only at night.
5. Copy and typography:
   - zh print numbers use Chinese numerals (第十四图).
   - Credit date and institution go on their own line; en previously ran on as "Driving Rain c. 1833–34".
   - `text-wrap: pretty` on caption text to avoid orphaned last characters.
6. Portrait captions hide the employer descriptor visually (screen readers still get it). ja plates drop from 277 to 258 px; all are now about 30 svh tall.
7. Static tier at night: the chapter stills sit under the same lapis veil as the film.

## Remaining gaps
- **Full-page scrub cost**: a bug in `core/frame.ts` (WP-00) starts extra animation-frame loops when a frame is requested during a tick. WP-07's works section triggers it by calling `stage.requestFrame()` inside a ScrollTrigger update: 2,790 rAF calls per second at 4× on 390×844, and the film stalls. Logged as R7 (updated), R10 and R11.
- **Peak film memory** on SwiftShader with software decode: HD is +59 MB after load (within the file + 60 MB budget) but peaks at +89 MB while scrubbing. Portrait: +54 / +71 MB.
- **Composited layers in the film stage**: 10 by day, 12 with rain, against the budget of 8. The seven caption plates stay composited so every chapter stays readable to screen readers (logged in R12).
- **CSS bundle**: 29.5 KB gzip against a 28 KB budget; road's stylesheet is about 7.5 KB of it (R12).
- **Fonts**: 加 and 美 still fall back to a system face until WP-11 re-runs `npm run fonts` (R1 updated).
- **Narrow landscape screens**: at 4:3 and phone landscape the caption plate covers part of the scene. Hara's Fuji peak is just clear at 1024×768 but covered at 844×390.
- **iOS**: the Low Power Mode rescue is tested only by emulation and needs a real device.
- The optional 生/版 live-render toggle is not built.
