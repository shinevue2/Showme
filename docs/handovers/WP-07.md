## Fixes made (WP-07 files only)
1. **Night gold looked dead grey** (mean RGB about 70,68,62). I relit the room with the warm bounce of one andon and added a broad sheen around the candle highlight.
2. **The leaf grid read as a checkerboard.** Less tilt and anisotropy per leaf, softer cut edges, warmer double-leaf seams, and a falloff on the shoji reflection so it no longer shows a flat band.
3. **The gold shader was compiled twice.** A second variant for the post render target was linked on the first visible frame, which stalls. It now compiles during warm-up, and the material is forced to one pass.
4. **About 2 frames per second at idle.** The candle drifted back to its rest spot when the pointer left. It now returns only when the screen moves.
5. **Labels overlapped the screen and sat on a ragged line.** They now sit under the lowest corner of each panel, on one rail, and the camera lifts slightly during the dolly.
6. **Mobile fetched and registered the view chunk.** It now loads only outside the mobile layout and re-syncs when the window crosses 768 px.
7. **Results tables hid the "after" column on phones.** Below 600 px each row stacks, with the column names shown.
8. **Labels collided in 6 diagrams**, in all three languages. I moved labels and added a paper-coloured mask behind labels that lines pass under.
9. **zh titles broke mid-word** ("黄 / 金路径"). Titles now balance their lines, and zh keeps words whole.
10. **Theme switch:** the theme's bokashi wipe also moved the `case-*` names. They are now dropped during the wipe.
11. **The gold mist on the resilience cut-away looked flat**, and turned olive at night. It is now stepped Rinpa banks, with a denser gold at night.
12. **Uneven text and layout:** the walkthrough terms did not align, the dolly text ran too wide on large screens, and en labels squeezed the text on phones.
13. **Mobile gold panels:** I added a daylight band across the leaf and removed the yellow tint over the paintings.
14. **Smaller fixes:** the low-tier wear mask is cached instead of recomputed, and a never-shown `case__tate-mark` is removed.

The posters `works-byobu-{day,night}-1440.avif` are re-captured after the shader changes (29.1 / 27.2 KB).
