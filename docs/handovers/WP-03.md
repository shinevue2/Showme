## WP-03 implement

WP-03 is finished: the real film is encoded, published and checked, and the synthetic test film has been removed. `tools/film/verify.mjs --content --determinism` passes every check, `npm run typecheck` is clean, and a locked `npm run build` renders all 22 pages.

**What I changed before the final capture**
- **Calm caption zone (all 7 stations):** each landscape rest frame now has a bank of broad paper すやり霞 tiers on the right, with stepped half-ellipse ends sitting at 57–77 % of the frame width. In portrait the bank covers the top of the frame; fully calm paper reaches 27–32 % of the height. These replace the ragged soft-edged wall.
- **Crossings between stations:** paper cards sweep in carrying two stepped clouds (three in portrait) in a pale tint of the plate about to be printed. The arriving station's bank then takes over and withdraws, ground first. This replaced flat stripe frames and odd window shapes as cards left; at most one near-blank frame remains per crossing.
- **Outro gold:** the flat 山吹 capsules are now three Rinpa-style clouds made of seven stepped tiers. Their ends are long half-ellipses (width 1.9 × height/2) and the colour is plate 8 at 75 % over paper, `#FDC454`, which is within the plate palette. They rise from below while drifting right → left. The geometry is in `src/film/scene/end-bands.json` and the manifest.
- **Mishima fog:** each fog layer is limited to two or three tints and smoothed, so it reads as flat silhouettes rather than smeared blobs.
- **Hakone lake:** two tones instead of four stripes.
- **Nihonbashi framing:** moved right, so the left gate is a narrow edge, the bridge is centred in the open area and both gate posts frame it. `matchVp` changed as a result.
- **Bugs found and fixed:**
  - The sky and the mist bank of a neighbouring station were showing inside another station's frames while the camera moved away.
  - At rest, the portrait traveller was missing in chapters 3 and 7; he is now visible in both.
- **Pipeline fixes:** captures no longer get reloaded mid-batch by other agents' dev-server activity, and a master is written to a temporary file then renamed, so an interrupted capture can't destroy the previous one. Keyframes and stills are now exact to the frame (portrait 4.6 s and 13.8 s fall inside frames 110 and 331, not 111 and 332).

### Fixes made
1. **Gold clouds** (the "UI progress bar" look): each tier's ends now taper to a spear tip instead of a rounded pill end. The colour is now plate 8 at 50 % over paper, `#FBD78D`, which is the film palette's own tint. The tinted clouds in the crossings use the same ends. In portrait those tiers are slimmer, and each end is capped at a quarter of the tier's length so the shapes don't read as leaves or petals.
   - Off-frame ends were moved further out so no tip shows at the frame edge.
   - Files: `mist.ts`, `wipe.glsl.ts`, `end-bands.json`.
   - New `src/film/scene/end-band-path.ts`: plain TypeScript, no DOM or three, giving WP-06 the SVG path.
   - `verify.mjs` now checks against the new shape.
2. **Mishima**: a dark broken piece of the cedars stuck out between the mist lobes at rest. I removed the cedar layer (masks + regenerated plates); the cedars had no visible role otherwise.
3. **Hara under the desktop chrome**: the 60 px bar and ruler and the cover crop hid both Fuji's peak and the frame line at 1440×900. I zoomed the rest framing out: the peak now sits at 11.5 % of frame height and breaks the line at 16.5 %, with the sides inset 9 % (new `extras.frameLineInset`). Confirmed in the built site.
4. **Background colour bug**: the clear colour was untagged, so it rendered as `#FBFEFF` instead of `--paper`, and the edge of a sky was visible as a hard line during departures. It now renders exact paper.
5. **Station swap**: only one diorama is drawn at a time. They swap behind the full-frame paper wipe in each crossing, which allowed wider skies (no sky edges during camera moves).
6. **Code quality**:
   - `compileAsync` was compiling an empty scene; it now compiles the real one (`film.compile()`).
   - `dispose()` now also frees geometries and materials.
   - Removed dead code in the traveller module and a stray `__pycache__`.
   - Added a `--hide restmist` review option.

I then re-captured both masters, re-encoded, re-published and re-verified.
